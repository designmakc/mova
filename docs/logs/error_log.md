# Error log

> One entry per recurring or goal-critical error, newest first, coded per
> [../mechanics/error_taxonomy.md](../mechanics/error_taxonomy.md) (generated at setup).
> Tallied by **`node scripts/tally.mjs`**, which resolves `Root:`/`Reroots:` causation and
> drops `Retires:` findings — a plain grep of `Code:` gives the surface count only, and in
> the reference instance counted a symptom above its own cause for three sessions.
> Conventions: [README.md](README.md). Append via
> `node scripts/log-append.mjs error --file <body>`.

## 2026-09-25 — ERR-016

- **Error.** ✗ "a documentation" (×3: check items 1 and 3, and free production "I create a documentation for a new component") → "the documentation" / "documentation". Code: NOUN-COUNT ×3.
- **Interference.** Russian «документация» takes ordinary determiners and pluralises freely; neither held language marks a count/mass split at all, so there is no signal that English "documentation" refuses "a". This is the second kind of gap error — a system the held languages do not have — so the repair is the system, not a contrast.
- **Context.** lesson U02 SES-004 (2026-09-24), three sightings in one sitting, plus ERR-010's on 2026-09-22 — four in two sittings. Observed only, NOT scored: count vs mass is T-0009/U06, `pending` in topics.md; same convention as ERR-003, ERR-007 and ERR-010. Taken together with ERR-013's preposition misselection, U06 material is now being hit repeatedly in free production well ahead of its unit — routed to the review verb through SES-004's Next pointer as a possible resequence, which a lesson may not decide for itself.

## 2026-09-25 — ERR-014

- **Error.** ✗ "We designers a interface" / "Project go to review" / "The designer to finish a screen" → "We design…" / "The project goes…" / "The designer finishes…". Code: VRB-3SG ×3.
- **Interference.** none identified — Russian and Ukrainian conjugate every person, so the expected risk was under-marking English's one marked form (transfer.md, and ERR-003's prediction). Two of these three point the other way, which is the finding: the ending was added after a plural subject, omitted on a singular one, and in the third case no finite form was built at all. Errors in opposite directions are the signature of a **decision not being made**, not of a rule learned wrongly.
- **Context.** lesson U02 SES-004 (2026-09-24), graded check items 4, 5 and 7 (work/sets/2026-09-24_u02-check-marked.md). Repaired the same sitting as an explicit forward procedure — identify the subject by substituting he/she/it/they, then ending or base form, then the verb is finite and never "to" — and retested on three genuinely new items: 3/3, **same-day post-key**, exit condition **cleared** (work/sets/2026-09-24_u02-repair-marked.md). Free production an hour later produced "The team works" and "I create", both correct, the exact shapes of items 4 and 5. TAXONOMY: VRB-3SG's table row is written as omission only ("third-person -s dropped") and held none of this; ruled this session to widen the one code to both directions rather than open a sibling, because the diagnosis found one cause under all three — see error_taxonomy.md § Scope rulings.

## 2026-09-25 — ERR-013

- **Error.** ✗ "The team works of a new feature" → "The team works on a new feature". Code: PREP ×1.
- **Interference.** none identified from the held languages — **the interference was the teaching.** Earlier the same sitting the "X of Y" frame was taught as a fixed frame without being scoped to noun-noun relations, and it leaked into verb+preposition territory, where English collocation decides and no frame applies. docs/mechanics/teaching.md § "Absolutes have to be earned / scope your rules" is the rule broken; the class of words that breaks the frame was not named before the frame was written down.
- **Context.** lesson U02 SES-004 (2026-09-24), part 4 applied practice, free production (a work-chat status message, goal scenario 3). Verb+preposition collocation is T-0020/U06, `pending` — so the error is observed, not scored, and the repair is scoping the frame, not drilling the learner. Taxonomy significance: this is the **first misselection** case for PREP, where every prior sighting (ERR-006, ERR-009) was an omission. The PREP-MISS/PREP-CHOICE split flagged as a gap in ERR-006 now has one real instance of each; ruling appended to error_taxonomy.md this session. The same sentence's other halves were correct and are recorded as such: "The team works" carried both the article and the third-person ending on a collective subject, in free production with no cue.
