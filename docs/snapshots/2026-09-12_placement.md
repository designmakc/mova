# Placement snapshot — 2026-09-12

The first session's probe, run under `lesson` (the carrier verb in `focus: full`), across
U01–U03 of the curriculum before any teaching happened.

## Method

Probed across the curriculum's first three units (articles/sentence structure, present
simple/plurals, adjectives/comparison/reading skim) — 10 items plus one free-form
production sentence and one reading-comprehension check. Untimed, no aids. Marked against
standard, well-established English grammar (a/an/the, present-simple agreement, regular
and common irregular plurals, comparison) — this pack has no live dictionary adapter
(`packs/en/pack.md` § Dictionary reachability), so every mark below is the agent's word,
not adapter-confirmed; disputes are welcome and will be re-checked against
`packs/en/notes.md` and any tutor the learner brings on later.

Not exercised: listening (no audio channel on this machine), speaking (not a goal),
past tense, questions/negation, prepositions, spelling recall under dictation — all later
units.

## What this confirms or corrects against the setup guess

- **Confirms the self-report as a genuine true beginner in production**: no article system
  in active use, third-person *-s* dropped, irregular plurals not known — matches
  `docs/reference/transfer.md`'s predicted traps (ART-MISS, VRB-3SG) almost exactly, and
  matches the intake snapshot.
- **One correction to the setup guess**: reading comprehension is noticeably stronger than
  the production score alone would suggest — the design-system paragraph was read
  correctly with no simplification and no prompting, including a word (*consistent* →
  *консистентные*) recognised through a lexical cognate rather than taught. Recognition
  running ahead of production is exactly the asymmetry `docs/reference/transfer.md`'s
  one-rung-ladder section predicts, and it means U03's reading-skim work can likely move
  a little faster than U01–U02's production work once we get there — noted for the plan,
  not acted on yet.
- **U01 remains the right starting unit.** Nothing here suggests skipping ahead; the article
  and sentence-structure gaps U01 targets are exactly what showed up.
